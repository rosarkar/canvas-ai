The vision

100,000 community builders earning a living from the audiences they built.

Human attention has always been the most valuable commodity on the internet. Search engines were built on it. Social platforms were monetized by auctioning it. AI labs are now spending billions to collect and label it.

But the people who actually gather that attention, the community builders, the creators, the group owners who spent years earning trust from a specific audience, have never been cut in. Canvas changes that.

Replace the standard Telegram join check with a short sponsored task. The human gets in. The advertiser or AI lab gets a verified, contextual human response. The group owner gets paid in USDC on every single join.

No brand deals. No sponsorship pitches. No minimum audience size. The market for verified human attention runs continuously, and it should flow to the people who built that attention in the first place.

Two problems, one mechanism
01
Brand deals are collapsing. Community builders have no reliable income.

Automation is displacing knowledge workers faster than new roles appear. Creators who once relied on YouTube ad revenue or influencer sponsorships are watching payouts fall. Brands can't justify spend without direct conversion data, so the checks stop coming.

A creator with 80,000 Telegram followers and a deeply engaged community sits on real, recurring, verifiable human attention. But there's no mechanism to route revenue from brands directly to that person, per verified completion, without an agent or a negotiation.

The community builders doing the actual work of aggregating and maintaining trust across specific audiences are left out of the market they created.

02
AI labs are paying millions for labeled data. Most of it has no context.

Training large language models requires vast quantities of human-labeled data. Current pipelines, Mechanical Turk, Scale AI, and their equivalents, share a structural flaw: annotators have no verifiable connection to the subject matter.

A generic crowdworker ranking responses about DeFi lending is not the same signal as a verified DeFi trader doing the same task. The label looks identical in the training set. One annotator knows what slippage means. The other is clicking for a dollar.

What AI labs need is not just volume. It's provenance: labeled outputs where the annotator's context is part of the data record. That doesn't exist at scale today. Canvas is how it gets built.

"The CAPTCHA is a toll booth that collects human attention and pays it to nobody."

Every day, millions of people solve join verifications to enter Telegram groups. That attention is real, recurring, and currently wasted. Canvas redirects it.

The AI data opportunity

Canvas is the data supply chain AI labs can't build themselves.

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and every frontier lab training competitive models needs one thing they cannot manufacture: high-quality human-labeled data from people with verified domain expertise.

They can hire contractors. They can build crowdsourcing platforms. But they cannot create the communities. The 4,200-person DeFi trading group, the 12,000-person physician network, the 800-person competitive gaming community: these took years to build and are impossible to replicate on demand.

Canvas is the infrastructure layer that connects those communities to the AI labs that need their labels. Every Telegram group that registers becomes a node in a context-verified human data network.

A real human, already embedded in a topic-specific community, attempts to join a registered group.

📋
Step 2
AI lab task served in DM

🤖

AI scoring checks the response for genuine engagement before the completion is counted. Non-genuine responses are rejected before any budget moves.

⛓

Every completion is recorded on Base with group context, timestamp, and scoring outcome. The AI lab receives a labeled output with cryptographic provenance.

Scale

Telegram has 900 million monthly active users. Most groups do zero verification today.

The total addressable market is every Telegram group that runs any form of join verification. That includes community groups, creator fan clubs, professional networks, local interest communities, and every project or brand that maintains a Telegram presence.

Canvas does not require a crypto-native audience. A parenting group, a local restaurant community, or a fitness accountability group can register and earn. Crypto is the starting vertical because the payment rails are native. The long-term market is every community on the internet.
