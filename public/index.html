Why AI labs pay a premium

Valuable training data
isn't random.
It's contextual.

AI labs spend hundreds of millions on labeled data. The problem isn't volume. It's provenance. A DeFi trader ranking protocol responses produces a categorically different label than a generic crowdworker doing the same task.

Canvas is the only pipeline combining verified human identity, community-derived topic context, and a captive ambient attention moment in a single labeled output. That provenance is logged onchain on Base. It can't be claimed retroactively.

I
Verified identity

Every annotator passes a conversational AI check before admission. Bots and low-effort responses are filtered before any payout releases.

II
Community context

Annotators are already members of topic-specific communities. Demographic context is baked in, not estimated. You know who labeled it and why they're qualified.

III
Onchain provenance

Every completion is logged on Base mainnet with group ID, timestamp, and scoring outcome. Training auditors can trace every label to its source.
